Libraries and tools

  • torchaudio: loading, resampling, STFT, mel spectrograms, augmentation transforms.
  • librosa: classic audio analysis; excellent docs and display utilities.
  • ONNX Runtime: portable inference and static int8 quantization. PyTorch quantization docs cover the FX workflow.
  • TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers and ESP-DL: running int8 models on microcontrollers; ESP-NN provides optimised ESP32 kernels.
  • Ultralytics YOLO: object detection (AGPL-3.0). Open-vocabulary alternatives: YOLO-World, OWL-ViT.
  • pyroomacoustics: room simulation (image-source method), microphone arrays, DOA (SRP-PHAT, MUSIC, and others), beamforming.
  • Open3D: point clouds, RGB-D, plane segmentation, ray casting, visualisation.
  • PANNs: pretrained AudioSet CNNs, including MobileNet variants.

Key papers

Audio classification and edge models

  • Salamon, Jacoby, and Bello (2014). A Dataset and Taxonomy for Urban Sound Research. The UrbanSound8K paper.
  • Howard et al. (2017). MobileNets: depthwise-separable convolutions for efficient vision.
  • Kong et al. (2020). PANNs: Large-Scale Pretrained Audio Neural Networks.
  • Park et al. (2019). SpecAugment.
  • Jacob et al. (2018). Quantization and Training of Neural Networks for Efficient Integer-Arithmetic-Only Inference. The affine int8 scheme used everywhere.
  • Han, Mao, and Dally (2016). Deep Compression: pruning, quantization, and coding.
  • Banbury et al. (2021). MLPerf Tiny Benchmark: how to benchmark microcontroller ML fairly.

Self-supervised and cross-modal learning

  • Arandjelović and Zisserman (2017). Look, Listen and Learn: audio-visual correspondence as self-supervision.
  • Aytar, Vondrick, and Torralba (2016). SoundNet: transferring visual knowledge into audio with unlabelled video. The closest ancestor of Module 2.
  • Chen et al. (2020). SimCLR, and Saeed, Grangier, and Zeghidour (2021). COLA: contrastive learning for images and for audio.
  • Xie et al. (2020). Self-training with Noisy Student: the teacher-student loop with strong augmentation.
  • Arazo et al. (2020). Pseudo-Labeling and Confirmation Bias in Deep Semi-Supervised Learning.

Localization and room acoustics

  • Knapp and Carter (1976). The Generalized Correlation Method for Estimation of Time Delay. The origin of GCC-PHAT.
  • Allen and Berkley (1979). Image Method for Efficiently Simulating Small-Room Acoustics.
  • Scheibler, Bezzam, and Dokmanić (2018). Pyroomacoustics.
  • Adavanne et al. (2019). Sound Event Localization and Detection of Overlapping Sources Using Convolutional Recurrent Neural Networks (SELDnet).
  • Chen et al. (2020). SoundSpaces: Audio-Visual Navigation in 3D Environments.
  • Senocak et al. (2018). Learning to Localize Sound Source in Visual Scenes: the learned visual heatmaps that Module 3's physics layer checks.

Glossary

Aliasing
Frequencies above the Nyquist limit folding back as false lower frequencies after sampling. See Concept 1.
Azimuth / elevation
Horizontal angle around the array, and angle above or below its horizontal plane.
Calibration (quantization)
Running representative inputs through a model to record activation ranges before fixing int8 scales.
Cosine similarity
The cosine of the angle between two vectors, \(u\cdot v / (\lVert u\rVert\lVert v\rVert)\). See Concept 8.
dB, dBFS
Logarithmic ratio: \(10\log_{10}\) of a power ratio or \(20\log_{10}\) of an amplitude ratio. dBFS is relative to digital full scale.
Depthwise-separable convolution
A per-channel spatial convolution followed by a 1×1 channel-mixing convolution; about \(k^2\) times cheaper than a standard one.
Diffraction
Sound bending around obstacles and through openings, strongest when the wavelength is comparable to the obstacle.
DOA
Direction of arrival: the direction a sound comes from, relative to the microphone array.
Embedding
A vector representation of an input in which similar inputs are close together.
Extrinsics / intrinsics
Extrinsics: the rotation and translation between two coordinate frames. Intrinsics: a camera's focal lengths and principal point, the matrix \(K\).
Far field
Source far enough away that wavefronts at the array are effectively flat.
FFT / DFT
The discrete Fourier transform turns \(N\) samples into \(N\) frequency coefficients; the FFT is the fast algorithm for it.
GCC-PHAT
Generalized cross-correlation with phase transform: delay estimation from the whitened cross-spectrum of two channels. See Concept 10.
Hop length
Samples between the starts of consecutive STFT frames.
Image source (mirror source)
A virtual source behind a wall whose straight-line path to the listener matches a reflection's path. See Concept 12.
InfoNCE
Contrastive loss: a softmax over similarities that picks the positive pair out of a batch.
k-means
Clustering that alternates between assigning points to the nearest of \(k\) centroids and recomputing the centroids.
Leakage
Any path by which test-set information influences training or model selection. See Concept 6.
Lift / PMI
How much more often two events co-occur than if they were independent; used to link audio clusters to visual tags.
Log-mel spectrogram
Power spectrogram pooled into mel-spaced bands and converted to dB; the lab's model input. See Concept 3.
MAC
Multiply-accumulate, the basic unit of neural network compute.
Macro-F1
F1 score averaged equally over classes, so rare classes count as much as common ones.
Mel scale
A perceptual warping of frequency, roughly linear below 1 kHz and logarithmic above.
MFCC
Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients: a DCT of log-mel energies, a compact classical feature.
Non-maximum suppression (NMS)
Removing overlapping duplicate detections, keeping the most confident one.
Nyquist frequency
Half the sample rate; the highest frequency a sampled signal can represent.
Occlusion
A source hidden from the camera (or from the direct acoustic path) by a wall or object.
Point cloud
A set of 3-D points, here obtained by back-projecting a depth image.
Precedence effect
Perceiving direction from the first arrival and suppressing echoes that follow shortly after.
Pruning (structured / unstructured)
Removing whole channels or filters, versus zeroing individual weights.
Pseudo-label
A label generated automatically (here, from visual co-occurrence or a teacher model) and used for training.
PTQ / QAT
Post-training quantization (no retraining, needs calibration) versus quantization-aware training (simulated rounding during fine-tuning).
RANSAC
Robust model fitting by repeatedly fitting to random minimal subsets and keeping the model with the most inliers.
RIR
Room impulse response: what a microphone records from an ideal click in a room; convolving with it adds that room's reverberation.
RT60
Time for reverberation to decay by 60 dB.
Scale / zero point
The two parameters of affine quantization: \(r \approx s\,(q - z)\). See Concept 7.
SNR
Signal-to-noise ratio, \(10\log_{10}(P_s/P_n)\) in dB.
SpecAugment
Augmentation that masks random frequency bands and time spans of a spectrogram.
Spatial aliasing
Ambiguous direction estimates for frequencies whose half-wavelength is shorter than the mic spacing.
SRP-PHAT
Steered response power with phase transform: GCC-PHAT summed over all mic pairs for each candidate direction.
STFT
Short-time Fourier transform: FFTs of overlapping windowed frames, giving a time-frequency representation.
TDOA
Time difference of arrival of a sound between two microphones.
